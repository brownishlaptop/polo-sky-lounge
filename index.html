01 — THE POLO STORY

More than a night out.
A place to belong.

Polo Lounge brings a new kind of energy to Ikorodu: a polished all-day escape designed around good food, great music and better company.

Meet friends over a plate, settle in at the bar, take in the rooftop views or stay late when the lounge finds its rhythm. Every visit has a different mood — but it always feels like Polo.
